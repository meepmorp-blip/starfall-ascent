`setting: orphanage`

*And everything stops.*

*Only the man in the cloak keeps moving.*
*In the silence there is one sound left. Tick. Tick. Tick. Like a clock inside the stopped minute.*
*The bundle in his arms squirms. The baby is awake. Its face scrunches up at the ticking, and it starts to cry.*

`setting: orphanage`

**The man in the black cloak:** I’m sorry. You’ll have to do this part without me.
**The man in the black cloak:** Don’t drink the water.
**The man in the black cloak:** And this time… finish it.

`setting: orphanage`
