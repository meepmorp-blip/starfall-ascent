# STARFALL: THE ASCENT
## Chapter 1: "The Minute Meridian Held Its Breath"
*Full script, draft 1 · working names · ~35 minutes with gameplay*

**Format**
- `NAME:` spoken line. *(italics)* = action or tone.
- **[CUTSCENE]** = illustrated panels / scripted camera, no control.
- **[GAMEPLAY]** = playable segment, with goal and length.
- **[BOND]** = camp choice. Pick who to talk to; each talk raises that character's Bond.
- Party grows during the chapter: Kairos → +Ines, Bram → +Noemi, Theo → +Lucan, Dess → +Cass.
- Lyra is a story character only (not playable).

## COLD OPEN · "Twenty Years Ago"

**[CUTSCENE]** *Rain over Meridian. Night. Clock towers everywhere, all lit.*

*A street. A woman stops mid-sentence, hand raised to call someone. She lowers it. She can't remember who.*

*A bakery owner stares at his own sign as if he's never seen it.*

*A MAN IN A LONG COAT runs through it all. We never see his face. He holds a bundle of papers against his chest. Pages slip loose and melt into ink in the rain.*

MAN: *(breathless, to himself)* Not yet. Not yet, not yet—

*The orphanage of St. Hesper. Above the gate, a huge clock. Its hands are crawling toward midnight.*

*He kneels and sets down a bundle: a NEWBORN, wrapped in a coat lining. The baby isn't crying. It's watching the clock.*

MAN: Hey. *(soft)* Don't look at it. You'll hate it later anyway.

*He tucks a folded page into the blanket.*

MAN: Find me before they do.

*The clock reaches 11:59. And stops. Every clock in the city stops. The rain hangs in the air.*

*The man fades from the edges inward, like a word being rubbed out. Then he's gone.*

*The rain falls again. A nun opens the gate, finds the baby, and looks up at the dead clock, confused.*

NUN: …What time is it?

*Nobody answers.*

## SCENE 1 · "The Boy Who Remembers"
*Present day. Morning. The Market Ring of Meridian.*

**[GAMEPLAY · walk, ~1 min]** *Kairos walks through the market. Light interaction prompts. Signs everywhere: prices painted big, names on doors, "TODAY IS: TUESDAY" chalked on a board. Everyone carries a small notebook (a Ledger).*

OLD MATTEO: *(holding out a notebook)* Kairos. Kairos! What did I write here? The ink ran.

KAIROS: "Pick up Rosa's medicine. Thursday." It's Tuesday, Matteo.

OLD MATTEO: Tuesday. Right. *(writes it again)* What would we do without you.

KAIROS: Write bigger.

*A FISH VENDOR waves.*

FISH VENDOR: How much did I charge you last week?

KAIROS: Same as this week. Don't try it.

FISH VENDOR: *(grinning)* Worth a shot.

*A small girl, TESSA, tugs his sleeve.*

TESSA: Mama says you don't forget anything. Ever. Is that true?

KAIROS: *(beat)* Most people forget a little. Some forget a lot. I'm just… on the other end.

TESSA: That sounds nice.

KAIROS: It's mostly heavy.

*The CLOCK TOWER above the square chimes nine. Kairos flinches hard, like someone slapped a door next to his ear.*

TESSA: You don't like clocks.

KAIROS: Never have.

**[VISION]** *Screen flickers white. For half a second: the tower crane above the fruit stalls snapping. Steel falling. Tessa looking up.*

*Back to normal. Nothing has happened. Kairos is staring at the crane.*

KAIROS: *(quietly)* Tessa. Go find your mom. Now.

TESSA: Why?

KAIROS: Please.

## SCENE 2 · "The Stopped Minute"

**[CUTSCENE]** *A cable snaps. The crane tilts. People scream. Kairos runs toward Tessa—*

*—and everything stops.*

*Sound gone. Falling steel frozen mid-air. A pigeon frozen mid-flap. Tessa frozen looking up. The colors drain to grey-blue.*

*Kairos is still moving. He stumbles, gasping. He looks around. Nobody is breathing.*

KAIROS: …Hello?

*A light moves at the end of the street: two shapes of light circling each other like fish, around a clock face with no hands.*

THE VOICE: *(calm, warm, oddly familiar)* You can breathe. You're the only one who can, right now.

KAIROS: What is this? Who are you?

THE VOICE: Someone you'll recognize. Not today.

KAIROS: That's not an answer.

THE VOICE: It's the only honest one I have.

*The two lights split and fly at him. They hit his hands and become TWO BLADES, curved, silver-blue, like fins.*

THE VOICE: You hate clocks because you know what they're for. They measure what can be taken from you.

THE VOICE: This minute is yours. Use it.

*The light turns and drifts away down the street.*

KAIROS: Wait— hey!

**[GAMEPLAY · "Chase the Voice", ~1 min]** *Run through the frozen market. Weave between floating debris and frozen people. Kairos can push frozen objects out of the way. Tutorial prompts: MOVE, DODGE (dodge through narrow gaps). Before the end, he pushes the falling steel beam aside so it will miss Tessa.*

*He reaches the light. It looks at him, if light can look.*

THE VOICE: You were there.

*Snap. Color and noise slam back.*

## SCENE 3 · "Meridian Holds Its Breath"

**[CUTSCENE]** *The beam crashes harmlessly beside Tessa. Everyone in the square drops to their knees, gasping, as if they'd been underwater.*

*All over the city: people clutching their chests. Clocks restarting. A dog barking.*

TESSA'S MOTHER: *(grabbing Tessa)* I couldn't breathe— I couldn't—

*Above the square the air TEARS. A glowing crack in the sky, like paper ripping. A RIFT.*

*Things crawl out. Shapes of smoke and ash with red-hot eyes. VICES.*

*Kairos looks down. He's still holding the blades.*

KAIROS: *(very quietly)* Okay. Okay.

*A Vice lunges at a stall. Kairos moves before he decides to. His body knows the stance.*

**[GAMEPLAY · first fight, ~2 min]** *Kairos solo. Tutorial-in-story: ATTACK (Twin Fish combo), FINISHER (5th hit), DODGE (perfect dodge shows Slipstream), TIDE stacks (slow → Drowsy). His Foresight passive is introduced: an enemy's attack flickers early.*

KAIROS: *(after the first kill, staring at his hands)* How do I know how to do this?

*More Vices pour out. Too many.*

## SCENE 4 · "Garrison"

*A gunshot cracks from a rooftop. A Vice drops.*

INES: *(over a loudspeaker, flat)* Civilian with the swords. Get down or keep fighting, but pick one.

KAIROS: *(looking up)* Who—

*A huge man in army gear vaults the fruit stall, catches his boot on a crate, and goes down shield-first onto a Vice. It flattens.*

BRAM: *(muffled, from the ground)* I'm okay!

INES: You're always okay, Bram.

BRAM: *(getting up, to the crate)* Sorry.

KAIROS: Did he just apologize to a box?

INES: He does that. Bram, cover the kids.

BRAM: Behind me, everybody! Behind the big guy!

**[GAMEPLAY · party of 3, ~2 min]** *Kairos + Ines + Bram. Tutorial: SWITCH characters (Entrance moves), Ines's Spotted marks and her off-field Overwatch, Bram's Stagger → stun, Sun and Moon skills, the Aspect combo.*

*The rift seals with a sound like a book slamming. Silence. Smoke.*

INES: *(climbing down, rifle on her shoulder)* Sergeant Ines Calder, Meridian Garrison. You're not army.

KAIROS: I fix— *(stops)* I don't fix anything. I just live here.

INES: Where'd you get those?

KAIROS: You wouldn't believe me.

INES: Try me. I've had a strange morning.

*Kairos's chest begins to glow through his shirt: a mark, two fish circling a clock face with no hands.*

BRAM: Uh. Sarge. He's glowing.

INES: I can see that, Bram.

KAIROS: *(swaying)* That's… new.

*He collapses. Bram catches him a second too late and they both go down.*

BRAM: I'm okay! He's okay! …I think he's okay.

## SCENE 5 · "Carry Him"

**[GAMEPLAY · escort, ~2 min]** *Bram carries Kairos (Bram can't attack, moves slowly). Ines is the active fighter; switch to Bram to body-block. Small rifts keep opening on the road to the hospital. Fail if Bram's carry meter breaks.*

BRAM: *(running)* He's light. Is he supposed to be this light?

INES: Eat less bread and you'll stop noticing.

BRAM: I'll never eat less bread, Sarge.

## SCENE 6 · "St. Lucia's"
*A crowded hospital ward. Half the city came in short of breath.*

*DR. NOEMI LARK checks Kairos's pupils. Her forearm is covered in neat handwriting.*

NOEMI: Pulse normal. Oxygen normal. *(glances at her arm)* Patient was glowing. I wrote that down because I knew I'd talk myself out of it.

INES: He was. Bright as a lamp.

NOEMI: People don't glow, Sergeant.

INES: Agreed. Write that down too.

*Kairos wakes with a gasp.*

KAIROS: The crane. Is the girl okay?

NOEMI: Everyone in your square is fine. Better than most of the city. What do you remember?

KAIROS: Everything. *(beat)* Time stopped. Everyone stopped. I was the only one moving.

*A pause. Bram looks at Ines. Ines looks at Noemi. Noemi sighs and writes something on her arm.*

NOEMI: Concussion, possibly.

KAIROS: I don't have a concussion.

NOEMI: That's what people with concussions say.

*The door bangs open. THEO LARK, hair wild, holding a device that's beeping.*

THEO: He doesn't have a concussion.

NOEMI: Theo. You're not allowed in the ward.

THEO: I'm your husband, I'm allowed everywhere. *(to Kairos)* At 9:04 this morning my instruments lost sixty seconds. Not a glitch. All of them. Every clock in the city lost the same minute, and the rifts opened right after. *(points)* And you were standing at the exact center of it.

KAIROS: …You believe me?

THEO: I've been waiting six years for something weird enough to prove I'm not crazy. You're my favorite person today.

NOEMI: *(to Kairos)* He means well.

THEO: I mean extremely well.

*The lights flicker. A rift tears open in the hospital courtyard.*

**[GAMEPLAY · hospital defense, ~3 min]** *Protect the patients' wing. Noemi joins (healer, ATK buffs) and Theo joins (turrets). The party is now 5; choose your active 3 at the start of the fight. Teaches choosing a team.*

*After the fight. Noemi sits down hard, exhausted. Theo sits next to her and quietly fixes her collar.*

NOEMI: You enjoyed that.

THEO: Parts of it.

NOEMI: *(small smile)* Me too. Don't tell anyone.

## SCENE 7 · "The Concord Office"
*The Concord Council's offices. Marble, filing cabinets to the ceiling, clerks with ledgers. Theo has brought them here to report the minute.*

CLERK: Name.

KAIROS: Kairos.

CLERK: Surname.

KAIROS: Don't have one.

CLERK: Birth record?

KAIROS: You have it. I've never seen it.

*A young man in a sharp grey coat walks past, reading a file, then stops. LUCAN CRANE. Cold eyes. Perfect posture.*

LUCAN: Is there a problem?

CLERK: Mr. Crane. This one has an irregular file.

LUCAN: *(taking the folder, flat)* Everyone's file is irregular this week. *(reads, stops)* …This is blank.

KAIROS: *(staring at him)* Lucan?

LUCAN: Do I know you?

KAIROS: *(it hurts, and he hides it)* We grew up in the same place. St. Hesper. You used to steal bread from the kitchen and blame the cat.

LUCAN: I'm sorry. I don't—

KAIROS: There wasn't a cat.

*Lucan's face doesn't change. His hand on the folder tightens.*

*The CHANCELLOR'S AIDE arrives with two guards and a man in a white coat: DR. EZEKIEL MARR. Friendly face. Reading glasses on a chain.*

MARR: Is this him? The one who doesn't forget? *(warmly)* Hello. I've been hoping to meet you since about nine this morning.

THEO: Dr. Marr. *(stiff)* We came to report the rift event, not to—

MARR: And I'm grateful. *(to the aide)* No Houses at all?

AIDE: None, Doctor. The Registry flagged him a long time ago. Unclassifiable.

MARR: A defect, then. *(kindly, to Kairos)* That's not an insult. It just means nobody's had the chance to look properly. I'd like to.

KAIROS: And if I say no?

MARR: *(smile doesn't move)* Then I'd have to ask the Chancellor, and he'll say yes for you.

*The guards step in. Bram moves forward. Ines puts a hand on his arm.*

INES: *(low)* Not here. Not like this.

BRAM: But he—

INES: I know.

*Kairos is led away. He looks back at Lucan. Lucan looks at the floor.*

---

## SCENE 8 · "The Lab"
*Dr. Marr's laboratory under the Institute. White tiles. And on the wall, a huge ticking clock.*

*Kairos is strapped into a chair. He can't stop looking at the clock.*

MARR: You really don't like that, do you. *(checking a monitor)* Your heart rate jumps every time it ticks.

KAIROS: Could you take it down?

MARR: Not yet. It's useful. *(sits, friendly)* Relax. I'm not going to hurt you. I just want to know why your head works and everybody else's doesn't.

*A young woman at the console: DESS MARR. Nervous, quick hands. She doesn't look at Kairos.*

MARR: Dess, bring me a core.

*Dess carries over a fist-sized, smoky crystal: a VICE CORE. Marr puts it in Kairos's strapped hand.*

*The core lights up. And a sound comes out of it. A child laughing. Then a woman's voice: "Lili, come inside, it's getting dark."*

*Dess drops her tablet.*

DESS: That's… that's a person.

MARR: *(delighted)* It's a memory. It reacts to him. Twelve years of these things on my shelves, and they've never made a sound.

DESS: Dad. That's a person's memory. Inside a Vice.

MARR: Yes, Dess, that's rather the point. Write it down.

*Dess stares at the core. Then at her father.*

---

*Meanwhile. A Garrison safehouse. Ines, Bram, Noemi, Theo.*

BRAM: We just let them take him.

INES: We let them take him so we'd still be free to get him back.

*A knock. Lucan at the door, hood up, holding a folded paper.*

LUCAN: The lab is under the Institute. Third sub-level. Guard change at eleven.

INES: Why help?

LUCAN: *(quiet)* There wasn't a cat.

*Beat.*

LUCAN: I remembered on the way home. The kitchen. The bread. The clock we used to hide under. *(his voice cracks once, then steadies)* I forgot him. How do you forget someone like that?

NOEMI: *(gently)* The same way everyone forgets everything. It's not your fault.

LUCAN: It's going to stop being my fault starting now.

---

**[GAMEPLAY · "Breakout", ~4 min]**
- **Part A (outside team):** Ines, Bram, Noemi, Theo, and Lucan (now playable) fight down through the Institute sub-levels.
- **Part B (inside):** Kairos alone, still in restraints until he triggers a short **time stop**: everything freezes for 3 seconds and he slips the straps. A light stealth puzzle: freeze time, walk past guards and camera sweeps.
- **Part C:** the two groups meet at a sealed door. **Dess** is there with a key card.

DESS: *(shaking)* My father's going to hate me.

KAIROS: Then why?

DESS: Because that core had a little girl in it. And he wanted to know if he could make more.

*She opens the door. Then she grabs a satchel of cores off the shelf.*

DESS: And I'm taking these. They're people.

*Dess joins. She releases one core: a soft blue figure of light, a little girl, who looks around, waves, and fights beside them.*

**[GAMEPLAY · escape fight, ~1 min]** *Dess's spirits introduced. Lucan's Vow mechanic triggers the first time you switch from Lucan to Kairos.*

LUCAN: *(to Kairos, as they run)* I'm sorry.

KAIROS: For what?

LUCAN: For forgetting you.

KAIROS: You came back.

LUCAN: *(flat)* That's the minimum.

## SCENE 9 · "The Inquisitor"
*Rooftops. Night. Bells.*

*A figure in grey and red blocks the path, swinging a chained iron censer. Fire drips from it. SISTER CASS VIRE.*

CASS: By order of the Office of Correction, the Unrecorded comes with me. The rest of you can go home.

INES: That's generous.

CASS: It's not an offer.

**[GAMEPLAY · mini-boss: Sister Cass, ~2 min]** *A fight against a future party member. She punishes standing still. Her burning censer leaves fire on the ground.*

*Mid-fight, a voice from the street below. FATHER AMBROSE VANE, older, calm, robes immaculate.*

VANE: Sister. The building beneath them is a lodging house.

CASS: Yes, Father.

VANE: Burn it.

CASS: *(beat)* There are people inside.

VANE: Faithful people, who'll be forgiven. Burn it.

*Cass hesitates. Too long. Vane sighs and throws a flask himself. The roof catches fire.*

KAIROS: No—

**[GAMEPLAY · 20 seconds]** *Kairos's time stop: carry three civilians out of the frozen fire. If time runs out, the scene still continues (no fail), but the dialogue changes slightly.*

*Time snaps back. The people are safe on the street. Cass is staring at Kairos.*

CASS: You could've run.

KAIROS: So could you.

*Cass looks down at Vane, then back at them. She lowers the censer.*

CASS: Go. Before I remember my orders.

## SCENE 10 · "The Clock Tower" (CAMP)
*The abandoned clock tower of St. Hesper's orphanage. The same dead clock from the cold open. Rain outside.*

*Everyone is exhausted. Bram is making toast over a lantern. Theo is fixing a drone. Noemi is checking everyone's cuts.*

THEO: So. Recap. Time stopped. You got magic fish swords. Rifts. A Vice core sang. The Church wants you. The government wants you. And you're the only person in the city who doesn't forget anything.

KAIROS: When you say it like that it sounds bad.

THEO: It sounds AMAZING.

NOEMI: Theo.

THEO: Amazing and bad.

**[BOND · choose two]**

**A. Lucan, at the clock face.**
*They sit on the narrow ledge behind the giant dead clock, like when they were kids.*
LUCAN: We used to hide here when the matron did inspections.
KAIROS: You'd make me count the seconds so we knew when to come out.
LUCAN: …You hated it.
KAIROS: I hated it. I did it anyway.
LUCAN: *(long pause)* I'm not good at this. Saying things.
KAIROS: I know.
LUCAN: So I'll say it once. Whatever this is, the Church, my father, whatever comes out of those rifts. I'm not losing you twice.
KAIROS: That sounds like a vow.
LUCAN: It is one. Don't make it weird.

**B. Ines and Bram, by the lantern.**
BRAM: Sarge saved my life in the Eastern Rift.
INES: You fell into the Eastern Rift.
BRAM: And you saved my life.
INES: *(to Kairos)* He followed me in. I told him to stay, he didn't. Fourteen Vices. He had a shield and no plan.
BRAM: I had a plan. The plan was you.
INES: *(looks away, almost smiling)* Eat your toast, Corporal.

**C. Noemi and Theo, at the window.**
NOEMI: *(quiet)* I wrote "he glowed" on my arm because I was scared I'd decide it didn't happen.
THEO: You always say the scary thing first.
NOEMI: And you always say the exciting thing first.
THEO: Between us we say everything.
NOEMI: *(to Kairos)* If this gets dangerous, and it will, I'm not leaving him behind. And I'm not leaving you behind either. That's not kindness. I'm just stubborn.

**D. Dess, alone with the cores.**
DESS: I can hear them a little. The memories. Mostly ordinary stuff. Someone's bad day. A song. *(beat)* My dad used to sing to me. I don't remember the song anymore. Isn't that funny?
KAIROS: What did it sound like?
DESS: I don't… *(shakes head)* If I find it in one of these, I'll let you know.

**E. Kairos, alone, touching the dead clock.**
*Vision. A man's hand, writing on a page by candlelight. The same handwriting as the folded page from the cold open. A voice, his own but older: "Find me before they do."*
*He snaps out of it. His hand is still on the clock.*

---

*Late. Everyone asleep. On a beam above them sits a young woman in a dark courier's coat, watching. LYRA.*

*Kairos opens his eyes. Sees her.*

LYRA: Go back to sleep.

KAIROS: Who are you?

LYRA: Someone who's supposed to be watching. Not talking.

KAIROS: You're bad at it.

LYRA: *(beat)* The festival tomorrow. Don't drink anything they give you.

*She drops from the beam and is gone through the window. Kairos is the only one who saw her.*

## SCENE 11 · "Festival of the Mercy"
*Festival Square. Banners. Music. The Church's annual festival. Priests hand out cups of clear water from silver bowls.*

PRIEST: Drink, and be forgiven. Drink, and let it go.

*A MOTHER drinks, smiles peacefully, and looks down at the toddler holding her skirt as if he's a stranger.*

MOTHER: …Whose child is this?

*Cass stands at the bowls in her grey and red, ladle in hand. She sees it. She sets the ladle down.*

VANE: *(beside her, gently)* Sister. The line is waiting.

CASS: She forgot her son.

VANE: She forgot her pain. Her son came with it. That's mercy.

CASS: *(very quiet)* That's not mercy. That's a theft with a hymn over it.

*She unhooks her censer from the altar. Lights it.*

VANE: Cass.

CASS: I've taken my vows seriously my whole life, Father. That's exactly why I can't stay.

*She walks away from him, through the crowd, to Kairos and the others.*

CASS: I don't want to hear "told you so."

KAIROS: I wasn't going to.

THEO: I was.

NOEMI: He wasn't.

*Overhead, the moon begins to darken. An ECLIPSE. Every clock in the square stops for one second. The crowd gasps. Then the sky above the festival splits into five rifts at once.*

**[GAMEPLAY · Festival battle, ~4 min]** *Cass joins (healer + fire). The first time the full roster of 8 is available: choose your 3. Waves from five rifts. Civilians to protect. Mid-fight, Kairos's Foresight shows something new: all of the Vices turning to face the same direction, toward the REGISTRY.*

THEO: *(looking at his device)* They're not attacking. They're migrating. Everything that came through is heading for the Registry.

INES: What's in the Registry?

LUCAN: Every birth record in Meridian. *(beat)* And his blank one.

## SCENE 12 · "The Registry"
*Night. The Registry of Births: a black stone building, endless windows. Guards everywhere.*

LUCAN: *(holding a ring of keys)* My father's. He doesn't know I took them.

KAIROS: He will.

LUCAN: Then I'd better make it worth it.

**[GAMEPLAY · "The Stacks", ~4 min]** *A descent through the Registry: shelves taller than buildings, flying paper, rubber stamps the size of cars. Split party: Team A (three chosen characters) and Team B (three others) take two routes; switch between teams at checkpoints. Ends at a vault door marked INTAKE WINDOW 1.*

*Kairos touches the vault. Another vision: himself, older, standing at a window with a long queue behind him, shouting at someone he can't see.*

KAIROS: *(shaken)* I've been here.

DESS: You've never been here. Nobody's allowed down here.

KAIROS: I know. I've still been here.

## SCENE 13 · BOSS · "Intake Window 1"
**[CUTSCENE]** *The vault opens onto a vast hall. Rows of empty chairs. At the far end, behind a desk as big as a house, a figure made of moonlight in a clerk's coat, face a pale disc: HORAIOS, Kosmokrator of the Moon.*

HORAIOS: Intake Window 1. Take a number.

*No one moves.*

HORAIOS: Name. Hour of birth. Sign here. *(the voice is tired, endless)* Next.

INES: Is that… the Moon?

THEO: *(whisper)* Write this down. Somebody write this down.

HORAIOS: *(turning toward Kairos)* …No number. That isn't allowed.

**[GAMEPLAY · Horaios, 3 phases, ~5 min]**
- **Phase 1:** stamps and paper fans.
- **Phase 2:** the paper storm: spirals and filing walls.
- **Phase 3, "Processing":** Horaios stamps every character's chart. One by one the party freezes, grey, "processed". The player is forced to Kairos alone. The music drops to just a heartbeat.

KAIROS: *(alone)* Fine. My minute.

*He triggers the full time stop. Everything freezes. He runs at Horaios—*

*—and Horaios turns its head.*

HORAIOS: You're not the only one who's done this before.

*Horaios moves inside the stopped minute. A huge hand closes around Kairos.*

HORAIOS: Processing.

## SCENE 14 · ENDING · "Don't You Dare Forget Me"
**[CUTSCENE]**

*Lucan breaks free of the frozen grey. Nobody knows how. Maybe the Vow. He throws himself between the hand and Kairos.*

LUCAN: Not him.

*The hand closes around Lucan instead. Behind Horaios, a GATE of white light opens: the Moon Gate.*

KAIROS: LUCAN—

HORAIOS: Irregular. *(considering Lucan)* But acceptable.

LUCAN: *(being pulled backward into the light, calm, looking straight at Kairos)* Don't you dare forget me.

KAIROS: I can't. I never forget anything.

LUCAN: *(the smallest smile)* Good. Then come get me.

*The Gate closes. Horaios is gone. The hall is just a dark empty room. The others unfreeze, gasping. The ceiling starts to come down.*

INES: Out! Everybody out, now!

*They run. The Registry collapses behind them.*

---

*Dawn. A rooftop. The six of them, bruised and silent. Every screen in the city shows CHANCELLOR AUGUSTIN CRANE.*

CHANCELLOR: *(calm, grave)* Last night a group of heretics attacked the Registry of Births and destroyed it. My son Lucan was taken by them. If you see these people, do not approach them.

*Their faces appear on the screens.*

BRAM: He knows we didn't take him.

INES: He knows. He just doesn't care.

CASS: Welcome to the Church's side of things.

*Kairos is staring at the sunrise. His chest glows faintly.*

THEO: *(gently)* Kairos. Where did it take him?

KAIROS: I don't know. *(beat)* But I'm going to find out.

---

*Another rooftop, across the city. Lyra hands a folder to a HOODED MAN. On the folder: KAIROS · BIRTH RECORD · (blank).*

LYRA: He used it twice. The minute. Horaios moved inside it.

HOODED MAN: *(calm)* He's awake, then.

LYRA: He asked me who I was.

HOODED MAN: Did you tell him?

LYRA: No.

*The man turns. Under his hood, on his chest, a mark glows: two fish circling a clock face with no hands.*

HOODED MAN: Good. It's too early.

---

*Final shot. Kairos alone. VISION:*

*A white room. Rows of desks. Lucan in a clerk's uniform, sitting at Window 1, filling out a form. He reaches the line "NAME" and stops. Pen hovering. He doesn't know what to write.*

**CUT TO BLACK.**
