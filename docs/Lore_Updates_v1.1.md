## The Mercy [CANON]
- Twenty years ago, on the night of the cold open, a rain fell on Meridian. The next morning a fountain had risen out of the stones of what is now Festival Square.
- The Concord Church claimed the fountain as a gift from Heaven and the source of its authority. Its water is **the Mercy**: handed out at Sunday services, at the yearly Festival of the Mercy, and in silver cups at life events (adoptions, funerals).
- **The water is the curse.** It wears memory away. The more often someone drinks it, the more they forget.
- **The Demiurge** made the rain and the fountain. This stays hidden; do not name or hint at the Demiurge in Chapter 1.
- Replaces the v1 "Blotting" entry: the Blotting is not a single instant wipe, it is the rain plus twenty years of people drinking.

### Reveal schedule
- **Chapter 1: clues only.** Never state that the water causes forgetting.
- **Chapter 2: the reveal** that people forget because of the holy water.
- **Later:** the Demiurge is behind it.

## Kairos's divine self [CANON]
- Kairos's previous life (**life B**) died on the night of the rain. His **divine self did not**.
- **The man in the black cloak is that divine self.** He carried the newborn (**life C**, the current Kairos) to St. Hesper's and has watched over that body ever since.
- **His goal:** to return to his physical body. ("Then I go home.") Whether that means joining with the current Kairos or replacing him stays open; play the tension later.
- **He can only act on the world while time is stopped.** Outside the stopped minute he works through others (Lyra).
- **The Voice** (the clock made of light) is the divine self's light form. It gave Kairos its two clock hands, which became his blades. That is why Kairos's mark, the Voice, and the cloaked man's mark are all **a clock face with no hands**.
- **Kairos does not stop time himself.** The divine self holds the minute for him. Kairos believes it is his power at first; in Chapter 1 he starts to notice "someone is holding it with me".

## Setting [CANON]
- Meridian is the last free city on the continent of **Aster**. Past the Wall, nothing has been mapped in 80 years.
- Starfalls (Vices falling) happen every night; the Garrison's rooftop guns stop most of them before they reach the streets.
