## Characters
- **Sister Cassie Vire** [unchanged identity]: a vowed Sister and Office of Correction agent, sincere in her faith, drinking the Mercy every Sunday since six. She becomes a *former* agent at the Festival.
  - [CHANGE] Met in the bell loft of St. Hesper's clock tower, off assignment. She is reading the orphanage's Sunday spoon ledger for children brought in the week of the fountain night (a page has been cut out).
  - [NEW] She carries a small vial of the Mercy "for bad nights".
  - [NEW] She keeps a hymnal page with one underlined verse: "The seven who were set to keep the gates have forgotten their keeping." It's a hint only. No cosmology is explained.
  - At the Festival she covers for Kairos ("These two are mine") before she breaks.
- **Theo Lark** [NEW]: was on Marr's staff at the Institute about two years, eight years ago. He built the first Seam reader, refused to lend it for Marr's "living tissue" work, and resigned quietly. Marr made his work "fringe", which explains why nobody takes him seriously, and his six years. His decommissioned reader sits on Marr's shelf.
- **Dr. Ezekiel Marr** [NEW details]:
  - Collects the Garrison's Vice reports.
  - One line about "patrons" who see results before the Council; the organisation stays unnamed.
  - Kairos can step *inside* a core's memory, which no one has done before, and Marr starts treating him as retained indefinitely.
  - Watches the breakout from the observation gallery and files a "heretic break-in".
  - At the Festival he takes eclipse readings, unseen.
  - Not defeated; returns in Chapter 2.
- **Dess** [CHANGE in motive]:
  - She recognises "retained indefinitely" from a file she was never allowed to open.
  - She swaps the "little star" core for a blank on her own initiative, and hides the real one.
  - She chooses, with Kairos, whether the trapped cores are released or kept sealed (`c_cores_*`). Either way her forgotten childhood stays a mystery.
- **Lucas** [NEW]:
  - His intake page: "Lucas. Foundling. Discharged to the household of A. Crane", dated the morning after the kitchen fire.
  - The door choice on the morning he was taken: he knocks and promises to come back, or walks past (`c_lucas_door`; Chapter 2 rescue: one beat).
  - He finds the clock tower from his own memory ("Inspection's over at nine. Meet at the tower").
  - He leaves the Chancellor's house taking only his coat.

## World details [NEW]
- **Tickers:** Theo's paired brass boxes that tap short messages over the city's speaking-horn wires (the same network Crane broadcasts on). Slow, short, and easy to cut: the Office cuts the hospital wire on Day 3.
- **Courier passes:** senior Registry clerks can sign out passes for the records-courier lifts that run between the Office archive and the Institute.
- **The Registry** files records by House (a planet's sign over every aisle). Today's drinkers have warm folders stamped "Unburdened". Some people have two charts with different birthdays. Outgoing forms carry Mercury's sign and "FORWARD: ARCHIVE". There is no archive on the Registry's plans. These are clues for Chapter 2, The Archive; Ailoaios (Mercury) is not named.
- **The Festival of One Cup:** every cup is numbered, and a Registry clerk writes a name next to each number. The water comes from a cistern under Festival Square: vats, numbered silver cups, nothing provable.
- **The Unburdening:** people write a grief on a slip and burn it before drinking.
