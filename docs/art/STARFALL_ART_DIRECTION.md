# Art direction: Celestial anime fantasy, in detailed top-down pixel art

## Pillars
1. **Top-down, always.** The camera does not change.
2. **A city that keeps time.** Clocks, rings, dials and star charts appear in floors, signs and ironwork. Round plazas, radial paving, numbered things.
3. **Warm lives under a cold sky.** People and lamps are warm (gold, amber, cream). The sky, the Church's stone and the Order's spaces are cool (midnight blue, violet, silver). Every scene sets one against the other.
4. **Light tells the mood.** Each district has its own time of day and colour grade. Light falls from the top-left; shade gathers bottom-right.
5. **Readable first.** Detail lives at the edges of the map. The fighting floor stays calmer so characters, Vices and red telegraphs always read.
6. **Original.** References (CrossCode, Eastward, Hyper Light Drifter and similar) guide craft and mood only. No copied designs.
