## Keepsakes (new)
- One Keepsake = **120 Memories** to one character. It can't be used at Lv 20.
- All Keepsakes available today: 3 (Chapter 1) + 6 (quests) + 9 (Renown) = **18** = 2,160 Memories. That is enough to take one benched character from Lv 1 to Lv 7 (1,740) with some left over, or three characters to Lv 4 (600 each). It helps them catch up to the story party, but doesn't replace playing.

## Expected early pace (first two hours)
- **Chapter 1 (about an hour):** the story party reaches about Lv 5 to 7, and the first clear brings everyone else to Lv 2. You have about 250 Stardust and 3 Keepsakes.
- **Meridian (about 30 minutes):** +235 Stardust, +6 Keepsakes, Renown rank 4 or 5 (+9 Keepsakes, +320 Stardust, Mastery +2 for all).
- **Spending:** that funds every Lv 5 milestone in the party plus a few skill levels and Attunes, and lifts two benched characters to about Lv 5.

## Currency loop check
- Salvage returns at most 120 Stardust for a Lv 5 Star-born fragment that cost 250 to Attune: no profit.
- Renown, Meridian quests and records, star ratings and the Chapter 1 first-clear Keepsakes are all keyed claims. Each pays once, including after a reload, after restoring a backup of the same save, or on a second visit.
