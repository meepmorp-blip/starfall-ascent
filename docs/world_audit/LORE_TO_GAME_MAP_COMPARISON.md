## Chapter 1 locations that are not explorable
St. Hesper's (cold open, Scene 9), Ash Lane (Scenes 5 and 10), Concord Office (Scene 7), Institute (Scene 8), Ash Lane rooftops (Scene 10), St. Hesper's clock tower (Scene 11), The Stacks (Scene 13) and Registry Hall (Scene 14).

## Should stay story-only, for narrative reasons
- **The Institute:** restricted, and a place of trauma for Kairos and Dess.
- **Registry Hall and The Stacks:** destroyed.
- **The Moon Gate:** a doorway, not a place.
- **The Drowned Canals:** stay closed until Chapter 2 reveals the Gate leak.
